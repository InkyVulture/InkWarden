InkyVulture

InkyVulture (Inky), is the owner and developer of InkWarden. An artist themself, Inky was tired of always seeing stolen art plastered all over the interet and decided to try do something about it.

Corwen, Warden of Ink

Corwen is the little raven who protects your artworks and fights off art thieves (not really, but he wanted me to write that). He protects art like its shiny trinkets.

Vesper

Vesper is Corwen's partner and emotional support. They are very shy and don't like anyone being near them except for Corwen. Vesper is an Argentavis.
